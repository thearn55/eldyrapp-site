Support

Need help?
Email support@eldyrapp.com. Include your device type, app version, and what you were doing when the problem occurred. Never send your password.

Accounts and cloud backup

An account is optional. Sign in from Settings to create or restore cloud backups. On the first sync, carefully choose whether to keep the current device or use the existing cloud copy. The app continues working locally when offline.

Delete an account

Sign in, then open Settings → Account & Cloud Sync → Delete Account & Cloud Data. Confirm deletion when prompted. This permanently removes the account and cloud snapshot. Local workout and nutrition data remains on that device.

Back up local data

Use Settings → Data & Backups → Export Full Backup before clearing app storage or changing devices. Progress photos remain device-only and are not included in exports or cloud backups.

Barcode scanning

Center the complete barcode, hold the package steady, and use even lighting. The scanner verifies repeated reads before lookup. You can select a barcode image or enter the printed barcode manually when the camera cannot read it. Product data comes from Open Food Facts and should be checked against the package label.

Workout PDF import

The importer supports selectable-text PDFs with one exercise per line. Scanned or photographed PDFs require OCR before they can be imported. PDF contents are processed locally on the device.

Camera access

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a food barcode. Safari and iOS control camera permissions. Barcode-image selection and manual entry remain available if live-camera permission is unavailable.

Fitness disclaimer

Powerbuilding OS is a tracking tool, not medical advice. Stop training and seek appropriate professional guidance if you experience pain, injury, or concerning symptoms.
