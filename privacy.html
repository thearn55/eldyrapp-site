Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

Eldyr is a workout and nutrition tracking application. It does not sell personal information, display advertising, or use personal data for cross-app tracking.

Information you provide

The app can store information you choose to enter, including your name, email address, body measurements, fitness goals, workout plan, completed workouts, lift history, nutrition entries, water intake, settings, and progress photos.

Apple Health and Apple Watch

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Eldyr requests permission to read recent sleep, step-count, and resting-heart-rate information and to save completed strength workouts. This information may originate from a paired Apple Watch. Apple Health access is optional and can be changed in the Health app. Health records read by this integration stay on the device and are not uploaded in Eldyr cloud backups, used for advertising, or sold.

Local device storage

Most app data is stored locally on your device. Progress photos and Apple Health imports are stored only in local app storage. They are not included in cloud snapshots or JSON backups. Workout PDFs are parsed on your device and their contents are not uploaded by Eldyr.

Optional accounts and cloud backup

Creating an account is optional. When you create or use an account, Supabase processes your email address, authentication information, account identifier, and cloud snapshot. A cloud snapshot may contain workout, program, body-metric, nutrition, settings, and history data. It is linked to your account so the app can provide backup and multi-device synchronization.

Barcode lookups

When you look up a food barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to retrieve product information. Review product information against the package label before saving it.

Service providers

The app uses Supabase for optional accounts and cloud storage, Open Food Facts for barcode information, and content-delivery services for app libraries and fonts. These services may receive standard technical request information under their own privacy practices. Eldyr does not use advertising networks or data brokers.

How information is used

  • Provide workout, progression, nutrition, metric, photo, and backup features.
  • Show Apple Watch-derived health context and save completed workouts when authorized.
  • Authenticate accounts and synchronize the user's chosen cloud copy.
  • Return food information for requested barcode lookups.
  • Maintain security, reliability, and user-requested support.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you remove it through device or browser storage controls, delete the installed app, or overwrite it by importing or restoring data. Cloud data remains until you delete your account or the service is discontinued. Signed-in users can permanently delete their account and cloud snapshot from Settings → Account & Cloud Sync → Delete Account & Cloud Data. Local data remains available after cloud-account deletion.

Your choices

You may use the app without an account, decline or revoke Apple Health access, pause automatic cloud backup, sign out, export a backup, restore data, hide nutrition tracking without deleting its history, and delete your account and cloud data in the app.

Security

Cloud snapshots use authenticated access controls designed so each user can access only their own snapshot. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, but the app limits cloud access and keeps administrative credentials out of browser and mobile-app code.

Children

Eldyr is not directed to children under 13. Do not create an account or submit personal information if you are below the minimum age required in your location.

Fitness information

Eldyr is a fitness logging tool and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment. Consult an appropriate professional before making decisions that may affect your health.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or assistance with your data, email support@eldyrapp.com.
