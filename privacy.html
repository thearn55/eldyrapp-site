Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026

Powerbuilding OS is a workout and nutrition tracking application. It does not sell personal information, display advertising, or use personal data for cross-app tracking.

Information you provide

The app can store information you choose to enter, including your name, email address, body measurements, fitness goals, workout plan, completed workouts, lift history, nutrition entries, water intake, health-data imports, settings, and progress photos.

Local device storage

Most app data is stored locally on your device. Progress photos are stored only in the app's local photo database. They are not included in cloud snapshots or JSON backups. Workout PDFs are parsed on your device and their contents are not uploaded by Powerbuilding OS.

Optional accounts and cloud backup

Creating an account is optional. When you create or use an account, Supabase processes your email address, authentication information, account identifier, and cloud snapshot. A cloud snapshot may contain workout, program, body-metric, nutrition, settings, and history data. It is linked to your account so the app can provide backup and multi-device synchronization.

Barcode lookups

When you look up a food barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to retrieve product information. Review product information against the package label before saving it.

Service providers and technical requests

The app uses Supabase for optional accounts and cloud storage, Open Food Facts for barcode information, and content-delivery services for app libraries and fonts. These services may receive standard technical request information such as an IP address, browser or device type, and request time under their own privacy practices. Powerbuilding OS does not use advertising networks or data brokers.

How information is used

  • Provide workout, progression, nutrition, metric, photo, and backup features.
  • Authenticate accounts and synchronize the user's chosen cloud copy.
  • Return food information for requested barcode lookups.
  • Maintain security, reliability, and user-requested support.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you remove it through device or browser storage controls, delete the installed app, or overwrite it by importing or restoring data. Cloud data remains until you delete your account or the service is discontinued. Signed-in users can permanently delete their account and cloud snapshot from Settings → Account & Cloud Sync → Delete Account & Cloud Data. Local data remains available after cloud-account deletion so users can export or continue using it offline.

Your choices

You may use the app without an account, pause automatic cloud backup, sign out, export a backup, restore data, hide nutrition tracking without deleting its history, and delete your account and cloud data in the app.

Security

Cloud snapshots use authenticated access controls designed so each user can access only their own snapshot. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, but the app limits cloud access and keeps administrative credentials out of browser and mobile-app code.

Children

Powerbuilding OS is not directed to children under 13. Do not create an account or submit personal information if you are below the minimum age required in your location.

Fitness information

Powerbuilding OS is a fitness logging tool and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment. Consult an appropriate professional before making decisions that may affect your health.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or assistance with your data, email support@eldyrapp.com.
